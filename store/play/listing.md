## English (en-IN / en-US)

### App name
Eyedrop Scheduler

### Short description
Eye drop alarms that keep the right gap between drops. Hindi, Tamil, English.

### Full description
Eyedrop Scheduler reminds you to put in your eye drops — on time, every time, with the right gap between different drops.

Using several eye drops a day, perhaps for glaucoma or after eye surgery, is easy to get wrong. Eyedrop Scheduler plans the day for you and rings like an alarm when each drop is due.

WHAT IT DOES
• Rings like an alarm, even when the phone is locked or on vibrate, so a dose is hard to miss
• Keeps at least 6 minutes between different drops, so one does not wash out the last
• Schedules drops every few hours, a set number of times a day, or at exact times
• Taken, Snooze and Skip buttons right on the alarm
• Can also add your reminders to your phone's own Clock app
• Looks after more than one person: a parent, a partner, yourself
• Every alarm shows whose dose it is and which eye it is for
• Reports show how many doses were taken, with a summary you can share with your doctor
• Short how-to videos in English, Hindi and Tamil
• Works in English, हिन्दी and தமிழ்

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• Everything stays on your phone; nothing is uploaded
• Optional PIN or fingerprint lock
• Back up to a file whenever you want, and restore it on a new phone
• Works offline; only the how-to videos use the internet

Eyedrop Scheduler helps you follow the schedule your doctor gave you. It does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
