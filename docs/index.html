Privacy Policy

Eyedrop Scheduler · Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version

Eyedrop Scheduler keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, store or sell any information about you, and we could not read your medication history even if we wanted to.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in a private database on your device:

  • The names, relationships and time zones of the people you add
  • The medications you enter, their dosage, and which eye they are for
  • Your reminder schedules
  • Every dose and what happened to it — taken, snoozed, skipped or missed
  • Your language choice and app settings

All of it is held in the app's own storage area on your phone, which other apps cannot read. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

If you turn on the optional app lock, your PIN is stored as a salted hash in your device's system keystore (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain) — never in plain text, and never off the device. Biometric unlock is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Network use

In its current configuration the app makes no network requests at all. It works fully offline.

The app contains one optional, non-personal network feature: fetching public fundraising totals to display how many spectacle frames have been funded. It is switched off by default. If it is ever enabled, it only downloads public numbers — it uploads nothing, and sends no information about you or your medications.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. They are not push notifications, so nothing about your schedule is sent to us or to any third party. The app needs notification permission purely so your phone can alert you.

Sharing — only when you choose to

Two features produce a file, and in both cases you decide where it goes using your phone's normal share sheet:

  • Backup — a file containing all your data, so you can move to a new phone
  • Adherence report — a readable summary you may want to show your doctor

These files are created on your device and handed to whichever app you pick. We never receive a copy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party trackers or data-collection SDKs
  • No crash reporting that transmits your data
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a person's profile in the app to remove them and their entire dose history. Uninstalling the app permanently erases everything it has stored. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to send us.

Because the phone is the only copy, we strongly recommend saving a backup file (Profile → Backup) before changing or resetting your device.

Health information

The app helps you keep to a medication schedule. It does not provide medical advice and is not a substitute for your doctor or pharmacist. Any guidance in the app — such as the eye-drop technique page — is general information only, and your clinician's instructions always take priority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
